Rishabh Rastogi, Ph.D.

Few people have built leading-edge semiconductor processes and then translated that work into clear, boardroom-ready insight for billion-dollar investment decisions.

I bring deep experience in advanced materials and semiconductor R&D, including nearly three years at ASM International developing ALD processes for sub-3nm GAA and CFET gate stacks — first embedded at IMEC's 300mm fab in Leuven, then in Arizona. 7 patent filings. 6 publications. 4+ process transfers to HVM beta qualification at customer fabs.

Senior Process Engineer

ASM International · Leuven, Belgium (embedded at IMEC) → Arizona, US Jun 2022 – Mar 2025
+

  • Supported 4+ process transfers from alpha development to HVM beta qualification at customer fabs
  • Worked with customer engineering teams on sub-3nm device development, diagnosing thin-film integration issues in GAA flows
  • Co-inventor on 6 patent filings at ASM International (2024–2025)
